# ORION research report template

## Research question
What strategy rule are we testing, and why might it work?

## Data
Record the ticker or synthetic seed, date range, source, missing-data handling, and price-adjustment method.

## Rules
Describe the signal, when it is calculated, and when an order is executed.

## Costs
Record commission and slippage assumptions, plus costs not included.

## Results
Record total return, annualized volatility, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, trade count, and benchmark results.

## Limitations
Discuss look-ahead bias, overfitting, survivorship bias, corporate actions, and why past performance does not predict future returns.

## Conclusion
State what the experiment suggests and which additional tests are needed. One backtest is not proof of a profitable strategy.
